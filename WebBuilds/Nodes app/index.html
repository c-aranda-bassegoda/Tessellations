Tessellations

How to Use the Nodes Interface

Create your own tessellation by drawing edges, applying symmetry transformations, and then repeating your tile.

Quick Guide

  1. Choose a symmetry template. Start by selecting a symmetry template. The corresponding base square will appear with a set of fixed black nodes (You can't move these!) that define the starting structure of your tile.
  2. Add and move nodes. Use the add nodes buttons to add smooth or sharp nodes to the tile edges. Click on the edge and without releasing drag the node the position you want. As you work, the tool automatically creates the corresponding changes on the symmetric edge.
  3. Refine your design. Switch to Select Mode to reposition existing nodes and fine-tune your tile. Use the remove node button to delete the selected node. The curvature of smooth nodes can be adjusted using their red handles (which appear when selected). Tip: to make a smooth node sharp just drag the red handles towards the node.
  4. Complete the tile. Continue adding, moving, and adjusting nodes until you are happy with the shape of your tile.
  5. Tessellate. Click Tessellate to copy your tile and reveal its immediate neighbouring tiles.
  6. Decorate. Once your tessellation is complete, decorate the interior of the tile to create your final pattern ( ).

Tip: First design the edges of the shape without thinking what it might be and when decorating decide what you think your shape looks like! Experiment with different symmetry templates to discover different tessellations.
